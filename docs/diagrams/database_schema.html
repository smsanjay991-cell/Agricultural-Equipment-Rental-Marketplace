Key Entity Relationships & Foreign Key Constraints:
🔹 users (1) ──< equipment (N): Owner posts machinery listings (ON DELETE CASCADE)
🔹 categories (1) ──< equipment (N): Equipment classification (ON DELETE SET NULL)
🔹 equipment (1) ──< bookings (N): Machinery reservation records
🔹 users (1) ──< bookings (N): Farmer requests rental bookings
🔹 bookings (1) ──< payments (0..1): Booking financial transaction log
🔹 equipment (1) ──< reviews (N): User review and star rating feedback
